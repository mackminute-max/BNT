# Blood and Thunder — Ancestry Reference Bible
## Unified Biological & Visual Profiles — Canon v1.0

**Scope:** Erf / Vaern. This document defines ancestry independently of nation, culture, language, social class, profession, clothing, and race-team allegiance.

**Canon status:** The **inventory of 13 ancestries**, D&D-inspired physiology and longevity as a design baseline, distinct evolved Hill/Mountain Dwarves, adapted Forest/High/Sea Elves, and **amphibious Sea Elves** are approved. The numerical height/weight/lifespan ranges and biological clarifications in this edition were approved for v1.0. Temperament remains individual rather than ancestry-determined. Descriptive anatomical details not explicitly covered by approval remain working art guidance.

**Design rules:**
- Ancestry is not nationality or culture. No ancestry has a predetermined morality, intelligence, profession, personality, or team.
- Sex-specific descriptions describe **typical adult male and female morphologies**, not mandatory traits. Individual variation is always permitted.
- Height/weight ranges are **ordinary adult working ranges**, not hard limits. Extreme individuals exist.
- Temperament is described cautiously; social behavior and personality must be generated individually, not from ancestral stereotypes.
- The visual direction is **Dark Deco Retro Cartoon**: confident smooth outlines, strong silhouettes, angular graphic shadowing, expressive adult faces; **not pixel art**.
- Images should be generated from anatomical descriptions, then dressed using separate national/cultural references.
- Do not translate qualitative anatomy into numerical gameplay buffs or restrictions without a separate balancing decision.

# A01 — Human

## 1. Ancestry Overview

**Classification:** Human. Medium stature, upright biped, comparatively balanced limb-to-torso proportions; unusually broad variation in build and facial structure.

## 2. Physical Dimensions

**Typical adult dimensions:** Men 165–190 cm / 60–105 kg; women 153–178 cm / 48–90 kg. These are approved typical adult production ranges, not hard biological limits.

## 3. Male Anatomy

Typically heavier brow and jaw, broader shoulders and greater upper-body muscle mass; facial and body hair highly variable, including full beards.

## 4. Female Anatomy

Typically narrower shoulders relative to hips, softer average facial contours and greater fat deposition at chest, hips and thighs; body size and muscularity vary widely.

## 5. Shared Physical Traits

Five fingers and toes; rounded external ears; flat nails; human dentition; no intrinsic horns, tails or unusual skin structures.

## 6. Coloration & Variation

Full natural human range of skin, eye and hair pigments; straight, wavy, curly and tightly coiled hair; freckles, birthmarks and early graying possible.

## 7. Lifespan & Development

**Typical lifespan:** 65–90 years; exceptional individuals beyond 100. Adult stature usually reached in late teens; physical maturity in early adulthood; visible aging and senescence generally begin earlier than in long-lived ancestries.

## 8. Physical Capabilities

Generalists with considerable adaptability; no default superiority in strength, speed or endurance over every other ancestry.

## 9. Physiology

Omnivorous, primarily diurnal but flexible; ordinary air breathing and terrestrial thermoregulation; climate tolerance depends heavily on individual and acclimatization.

## 10. Temperament & Psychology

No species-wide personality preset. Wide variation in sociability, caution, ambition, patience and emotional expressiveness.

## 11. Visual Identification

Preserve broad diversity of faces, skin tones, ages and physiques. Avoid treating one ethnicity or nation as the default human appearance.

## 12. Character Generation

Use broad distributions for age, height, weight, hair, face and build; nationality is a separate field.

## 13. Art Production Guidelines

Neutral full-body and bust references; five-finger anatomy; do not assign national dress, skin tone or personality from ancestry alone.

# A02 — Orc

## 1. Ancestry Overview

**Classification:** Orc. Large-framed humanoids with deep chests, thick necks, broad hands and powerful jawlines; visible lower canine tusks are the key silhouette cue.

## 2. Physical Dimensions

**Typical adult dimensions:** Men 180–215 cm / 90–155 kg; women 170–200 cm / 75–130 kg. These are approved typical adult production ranges, not hard biological limits.

## 3. Male Anatomy

Often very broad through shoulders and upper back, with heavy jaw muscles, substantial brow ridges and pronounced tusks; muscular or heavyset builds both plausible.

## 4. Female Anatomy

Typically powerful shoulders and legs, strong facial architecture and prominent but often somewhat shorter tusks; female figures range from compactly muscular to broad and full-figured.

## 5. Shared Physical Traits

Two lower-jaw tusks (size variable); five digits per hand; ordinary plantigrade feet; rounded-to-pointed ear tips; no mandatory facial piercings or tribal markings.

## 6. Coloration & Variation

Muted green, gray-green, olive, umber and brown skin tones; dark hair common, lighter hair possible; scars and skin texture vary individually.

## 7. Lifespan & Development

**Typical lifespan:** 55–80 years. Growth generally faster than humans in adolescence; adult by late teens; aging visible in middle adulthood, with considerable individual longevity variation.

## 8. Physical Capabilities

On average strong grip and high force production; large size may hinder maneuvering in cramped equipment; agility and stamina depend on training.

## 9. Physiology

Air-breathing omnivores with robust teeth and jaw musculature; ordinary sleep and recovery requirements.

## 10. Temperament & Psychology

Do not equate imposing appearance with aggression. Individuals can be patient, timid, scholarly, affectionate, reckless or reserved.

## 11. Visual Identification

Keep tusks and broad jaw legible in portraits. Do not depict as boar-headed, permanently enraged or uniformly monstrous.

## 12. Character Generation

Vary tusk length, brow, build, complexion, age and expression; Northreach and Karthen orcs share anatomy but not nationality.

## 13. Art Production Guidelines

Natural facial expressions, correct hand anatomy, varied occupations; no automatic war paint, armor or violent posing.

# A03 — Goliath

## 1. Ancestry Overview

**Classification:** Goliath. Very tall, broad-shouldered humanoids with long limbs, massive skeletal frames and large hands and feet; unmistakable scale against humans.

## 2. Physical Dimensions

**Typical adult dimensions:** Men 210–245 cm / 125–205 kg; women 200–235 cm / 110–185 kg. These are approved typical adult production ranges, not hard biological limits.

## 3. Male Anatomy

Often barrel-chested with heavy shoulders and thick limb musculature; pronounced nose, cheekbones and jaw, but substantial facial variation.

## 4. Female Anatomy

Similarly towering with strong shoulder girdle, substantial legs and broad pelvis; on average somewhat less upper-body bulk but no delicate or miniature proportions.

## 5. Shared Physical Traits

Five digits; rounded or slightly tapered ears; generally sparse body hair; skin patterns may resemble mottling or natural stone-like markings, not literal rock plating.

## 6. Coloration & Variation

Gray, slate, pale taupe, cool brown and stony skin tones; natural darker mottling or patches; hair may be dark, gray or pale and often sparse.

## 7. Lifespan & Development

**Typical lifespan:** 70–100 years. Adult stature reached later than humans; gradual aging; size does not imply immortality or elemental origin.

## 8. Physical Capabilities

Exceptional reach and raw lifting potential; higher food and equipment requirements; not inherently slow or clumsy.

## 9. Physiology

Air-breathing terrestrial omnivores; large caloric needs and strong cold tolerance are plausible tendencies, not absolute immunity.

## 10. Temperament & Psychology

No compulsory stoicism or competitiveness; social behavior varies individually.

## 11. Visual Identification

Always establish a height scale. Avoid giant proportions beyond the specified range, horns, literal stone skin or mandatory tattoos.

## 12. Character Generation

Generate very tall stature with independent variation in frame, musculature, markings, hair and demeanor.

## 13. Art Production Guidelines

Full-body comparison with human reference; account for oversized chariot controls and clothing as production issues, not automatic game penalties.

# A04 — Hill Dwarf

## 1. Ancestry Overview

**Classification:** Dwarf — Hill population. Short, deep-bodied dwarves with wide torsos, sturdy bones, powerful calves and relatively short limbs; softer and more generous contours than Mountain Dwarves.

## 2. Physical Dimensions

**Typical adult dimensions:** Men 125–150 cm / 65–105 kg; women 120–145 cm / 60–100 kg. These are approved typical adult production ranges, not hard biological limits.

## 3. Male Anatomy

Broad chests and waists, rounded faces, thick forearms and powerful legs; beards range from modest to abundant; strength remains evident beneath softer outlines.

## 4. Female Anatomy

Notably full-figured and exceptionally buxom on average, with pronounced breasts, broad hips, rounded waist and thighs, while retaining compact, sturdy dwarf proportions; individual variation includes lean and athletic women.

## 5. Shared Physical Traits

Five digits, broad hands and feet, dense hair, strong brows and jaws; female Hill Dwarves never grow facial hair.

## 6. Coloration & Variation

Warm ivory, peach, ruddy, tan, amber and copper-brown complexions; hair from blond and auburn to dark brown, black and gray.

## 7. Lifespan & Development

**Typical lifespan:** 250–350 years. Adulthood around 40–50; aging slow; long-lived individuals may remain vigorous for centuries.

## 8. Physical Capabilities

Excellent stability, sustained effort and load bearing; less reach than taller ancestries; individuals vary in conditioning.

## 9. Physiology

Terrestrial omnivores; robust frames and broad tolerance for temperate conditions; no biological dependence on alcohol or underground living.

## 10. Temperament & Psychology

No compulsory joviality, hospitality or stubbornness. Temperament independent of body type.

## 11. Visual Identification

Distinct from Mountain Dwarves through warmer coloration and fuller silhouette, especially women; do not draw as short humans.

## 12. Character Generation

Separate height, shoulder width, waist/hip ratios, male beard growth, bust variation, age and hair features; preserve broad individual range.

## 13. Art Production Guidelines

Show full-body proportions and profile; no required guild symbols or Frosthall clothing; avoid fetishized poses despite pronounced bust morphology.

# A05 — Mountain Dwarf

## 1. Ancestry Overview

**Classification:** Dwarf — Mountain population. Short, compact dwarves with denser-looking musculature, heavy shoulders, thick necks and hard angular silhouettes; visually distinct from Hill Dwarves.

## 2. Physical Dimensions

**Typical adult dimensions:** Men 125–150 cm / 70–115 kg; women 120–145 cm / 65–105 kg. These are approved typical adult production ranges, not hard biological limits.

## 3. Male Anatomy

Powerfully muscled arms, chest and back, square jaw, strong brow and comparatively little softness; beard density and style vary.

## 4. Female Anatomy

Strongly muscled shoulders, arms, core and legs; comparatively straighter, firmer contours than Hill Dwarf women, without erasing normal female anatomical variation.

## 5. Shared Physical Traits

Broad five-fingered hands, sturdy feet, heavy brow and dense bone structure; female Mountain Dwarves may grow exaggerated sideburns and hair along the jawline and chin, but never full beards; no intrinsic stone skin or metallic features.

## 6. Coloration & Variation

Paler ivory, cool beige, ash-tan and muted pink complexions common, with individual darker variants; dark, red, fair or gray hair.

## 7. Lifespan & Development

**Typical lifespan:** 250–350 years. Adulthood around 40–50; long lifespan comparable to Hill Dwarves; ancestral separation does not imply different aging rules.

## 8. Physical Capabilities

High relative strength, grip and physical durability; short reach; fitness still varies.

## 9. Physiology

Terrestrial omnivores adapted over generations to reduced sunlight and confined environments; not blind or harmed by daylight.

## 10. Temperament & Psychology

No inherent severity, greed or industriousness; these are character traits, not biology.

## 11. Visual Identification

Square, compact, muscular silhouette and cooler/lighter complexion; avoid equating all dwarves with Mountain Dwarves.

## 12. Character Generation

Vary musculature, male beards, female sideburn/jawline/chin hair (or none), complexion, facial geometry and age while keeping ancestry markers clear. Never generate a full beard on a female Mountain Dwarf.

## 13. Art Production Guidelines

Neutral anatomy sheets without helmets, armor or guild iconography; compare directly against Hill Dwarf male and female silhouettes.

# A06 — Halfling

## 1. Ancestry Overview

**Classification:** Halfling. Very short humanoids with balanced, slightly rounded proportions, relatively large expressive faces and sturdy feet.

## 2. Physical Dimensions

**Typical adult dimensions:** Men 90–110 cm / 20–38 kg; women 88–108 cm / 19–36 kg. These are approved typical adult production ranges, not hard biological limits.

## 3. Male Anatomy

Compact torso, modest shoulder breadth, variable belly and musculature; soft-to-angular faces and diverse facial hair.

## 4. Female Anatomy

Small frame with varied waist and hip contours, soft or athletic builds and expressive facial structure; avoid childlike body proportions in adults.

## 5. Shared Physical Traits

Five fingers, five toes, humanlike ears with subtle taper possible; comparatively large feet, often with natural hair on tops of feet.

## 6. Coloration & Variation

Full humanlike pigmentation range; hair frequently thick or curly but not exclusively; eye colors diverse.

## 7. Lifespan & Development

**Typical lifespan:** 120–160 years. Adulthood in early 20s; slower visible aging than humans; adults must look adult regardless of height.

## 8. Physical Capabilities

Good balance and fine coordination are common; absolute strength limited by body mass, but exceptional individuals exist.

## 9. Physiology

Air-breathing omnivores; ordinary terrestrial physiology; no mandatory barefoot lifestyle.

## 10. Temperament & Psychology

No innate cheerfulness, timidity or appetite stereotypes; personality independent of stature.

## 11. Visual Identification

Adult facial anatomy and mature proportions; avoid portraying halflings as human children or gnomes with identical features.

## 12. Character Generation

Vary face, build, hair, age, posture and footwear; scale is key.

## 13. Art Production Guidelines

Include human scale marker; no mandatory rural costume or comedic expression.

# A07 — Forest Elf

## 1. Ancestry Overview

**Classification:** Elf — Forest population. Fine-boned, long-limbed elves with graceful posture, tapered ears, delicate facial architecture and a subtle fae-adjacent quality.

## 2. Physical Dimensions

**Typical adult dimensions:** Men 165–190 cm / 50–80 kg; women 160–185 cm / 45–75 kg. These are approved typical adult production ranges, not hard biological limits.

## 3. Male Anatomy

Slender, lightly muscled, narrow-waisted, refined features; slight angularity to jaw and cheekbones; often visually ethereal rather than fragile.

## 4. Female Anatomy

Fine shoulders, long neck and limbs, delicate wrists and ankles, softly defined facial contours and subtle curves; mature adult anatomy despite youthful appearance.

## 5. Shared Physical Traits

Long pointed ears, five digits, fine hair, expressive eyes; no mandatory antlers, wings, glowing tattoos or overt faerie appendages.

## 6. Coloration & Variation

Fair, golden, olive and woodland-brown skin; dark, chestnut, copper, blond or silver-toned hair; eye colors broad, sometimes unusually luminous-looking without actual emission.

## 7. Lifespan & Development

**Typical lifespan:** 600–800 years. Physical adulthood by early adulthood, social maturity may be much later; slow aging and prolonged adult appearance.

## 8. Physical Capabilities

Excellent fine motor control, balance and flexibility as tendencies; no automatic supernatural stealth.

## 9. Physiology

Air-breathing omnivores; adapted to woodland light and variable terrain; fae resemblance does not automatically grant magical abilities.

## 10. Temperament & Psychology

Temperament ranges from exuberant to austere; fae-leaning appearance does not dictate whimsical behavior.

## 11. Visual Identification

Elegant, delicate silhouette with long ears and subtle fae facial traits; avoid turning every forest elf into a winged fairy.

## 12. Character Generation

Vary skin, face, eye shape, hair, ear angle, build and age; nationality separate.

## 13. Art Production Guidelines

Show pointed ears and full adult face clearly; neutral reference without leaf armor or woodland costume.

# A08 — High Elf

## 1. Ancestry Overview

**Classification:** Elf — Desert population. Long-limbed, lean desert-adapted elves with angular faces, defined cheekbones, refined pointed ears and a dry, austere visual quality.

## 2. Physical Dimensions

**Typical adult dimensions:** Men 170–195 cm / 55–85 kg; women 165–190 cm / 50–80 kg. These are approved typical adult production ranges, not hard biological limits.

## 3. Male Anatomy

Lean muscle, long neck, defined jaw and cheekbones, relatively narrow hips and strong posture; not necessarily frail or aristocratic.

## 4. Female Anatomy

Slender, defined musculature, long limbs, narrow-to-moderate shoulders and refined angular facial features; adult variation in bust and hips.

## 5. Shared Physical Traits

Pointed ears, five digits, finely textured skin; no innate glowing eyes, desert scales or supernatural markings.

## 6. Coloration & Variation

Predominantly bronze, copper, olive, deep tan and brown complexions with darker pigmentation common; dark hair frequent, sun-lightened strands possible; varied eyes.

## 7. Lifespan & Development

**Typical lifespan:** 600–800 years. Physical adulthood in early adulthood, social maturity later; centuries-long lifespan and slow aging.

## 8. Physical Capabilities

Heat tolerance and efficient exertion in dry climates as broad tendencies; not immune to dehydration or heat injury.

## 9. Physiology

Air-breathing terrestrial omnivores; long-term desert adaptation, but water needs remain real.

## 10. Temperament & Psychology

No innate aloofness, superiority or isolationism; Zarashi political identity is not biological temperament.

## 11. Visual Identification

Dark, dry-looking complexion, sharp features and elegant lean silhouette; avoid pale generic high-elf default.

## 12. Character Generation

Generate diverse dark skin values, facial structures, hair textures, builds and age; culture is separate.

## 13. Art Production Guidelines

Neutral anatomy without robes, crowns or desert jewelry; no automatic magic effects.

# A09 — Sea Elf

## 1. Ancestry Overview

**Classification:** Elf — Sea population. Lean, athletic elves with lighter complexions, strong shoulders and backs, pointed ears and subtle aquatic anatomy; wind-weathered presentation is common but not genetically mandatory.

## 2. Physical Dimensions

**Typical adult dimensions:** Men 165–190 cm / 55–85 kg; women 160–185 cm / 50–80 kg. These are approved typical adult production ranges, not hard biological limits.

## 3. Male Anatomy

Wiry musculature, defined shoulders and chest, long limbs and facial features that may be more robust than Forest Elves.

## 4. Female Anatomy

Lean athletic shoulders, strong back and core, streamlined body contours and variable adult female curves; not a mermaid body plan.

## 5. Shared Physical Traits

**Approved amphibious physiology:** breathe air and water; subtle subtle paired functional gill slits on the sides of the neck, behind and below the ears; mild webbing between fingers and toes; pointed ears; five digits.

## 6. Coloration & Variation

Commonly pale ivory, cool rose, pearl, light gray-beige and muted sea-toned complexions, with darker natural variation; hair pale blond, silver, sandy, brown or dark; weathering and windblown styling are acquired.

## 7. Lifespan & Development

**Typical lifespan:** 600–800 years. Physical adulthood in early adulthood, social maturity later; slow elven aging.

## 8. Physical Capabilities

Strong swimming efficiency, breath control and underwater coordination; on land remain capable bipedal athletes.

## 9. Physiology

Dual air/water respiration is innate; functional gills must remain unobstructed underwater; freshwater and saltwater respiration are both possible; underwater vision is good, but pressure/depth limitations remain ordinary.

## 10. Temperament & Psychology

No intrinsic wanderlust, adventurousness or sociability; maritime traditions are cultural.

## 11. Visual Identification

Keep pointed ears, subtle gills and partial webbing visible; avoid fish tails, full-body scales, fins covering the face or obligatory blue skin.

## 12. Character Generation

Vary gill visibility, webbing, hair, complexion, build and age; track whether the character is wet or wind-weathered separately.

## 13. Art Production Guidelines

Provide front, side and neck-detail views; show functional gill placement and hands; no mandatory sailor costume.

# A10 — Gnome

## 1. Ancestry Overview

**Classification:** Gnome. Small humanoids with relatively large heads, prominent noses, expressive faces, distinct ears and compact but adult bodies.

## 2. Physical Dimensions

**Typical adult dimensions:** Men 95–120 cm / 18–35 kg; women 90–115 cm / 17–33 kg. These are approved typical adult production ranges, not hard biological limits.

## 3. Male Anatomy

Lean-to-round compact bodies, large expressive brow and nose, variable beards and unruly hair; often slightly longer limbs than halflings relative to torso.

## 4. Female Anatomy

Small adult proportions, expressive facial architecture, prominent ears and nose, varied curves and musculature; avoid doll-like child anatomy.

## 5. Shared Physical Traits

Five fingers and toes, prominent external ears, humanlike nails and teeth; no intrinsic mechanical implants or goggles.

## 6. Coloration & Variation

Humanlike range of pigmentation; vivid red, brown, blond, black and gray hair all plausible; freckles and unusual eye shades possible.

## 7. Lifespan & Development

**Typical lifespan:** 350–500 years. Adulthood around 35–45; slow aging and long lifespan.

## 8. Physical Capabilities

Fine hand coordination and quick movements may be common, but inventiveness is not a biological trait.

## 9. Physiology

Air-breathing omnivores; ordinary terrestrial physiology.

## 10. Temperament & Psychology

No innate obsession with inventions, jokes or curiosity; those are individual or cultural characteristics.

## 11. Visual Identification

Differentiate from halflings through face, ear and nose silhouette, not mandatory hats or equipment.

## 12. Character Generation

Vary nose shapes, ear angles, facial hair, build, age and posture.

## 13. Art Production Guidelines

Adult face, five-fingered hands, no obligatory goggles, tools or whimsical costume.

# A11 — Goblin

## 1. Ancestry Overview

**Classification:** Goblin. Small, wiry humanoids with large mobile ears, sharp facial angles, relatively long forearms and expressive eyes.

## 2. Physical Dimensions

**Typical adult dimensions:** Men 95–125 cm / 22–45 kg; women 90–120 cm / 20–42 kg. These are approved typical adult production ranges, not hard biological limits.

## 3. Male Anatomy

Lean-to-muscular frames, angular jaws, broad mouths and pronounced noses; occasional stocky individuals.

## 4. Female Anatomy

Compact, agile-looking frames with varied muscle and fat distribution, slightly finer average jaw and brow; not necessarily dainty.

## 5. Shared Physical Traits

Five digits, large pointed ears, sharp canine teeth, humanlike feet; no compulsory claws, tails or oversized fangs.

## 6. Coloration & Variation

Green, olive, yellow-green, gray-green, ocher and brown complexions; hair dark, sandy, reddish or pale.

## 7. Lifespan & Development

**Typical lifespan:** 55–85 years. Early maturity, visible aging by middle adulthood; short-lived compared with dwarves and elves.

## 8. Physical Capabilities

Good maneuverability and fine coordination common; individual strength and endurance vary.

## 9. Physiology

Air-breathing omnivores; no innate aversion to sunlight.

## 10. Temperament & Psychology

No compulsory cowardice, trickery or cruelty; goblins can exhibit any temperament.

## 11. Visual Identification

Keep oversized ears, sharp face and compact adult build; do not render as childlike or feral by default.

## 12. Character Generation

Vary ears, nose, skin, build, hair and expression; Karthen citizenship does not change anatomy.

## 13. Art Production Guidelines

Neutral reference without rags, explosives or thief props; adult anatomy.

# A12 — Dragonkin

## 1. Ancestry Overview

**Classification:** Dragonkin. Tall, powerful bipedal reptilian humanoids with scaled skin, elongated muzzle, cranial horns or ridges, thick tail and plantigrade feet with robust clawed toes.

## 2. Physical Dimensions

**Typical adult dimensions:** Men 185–220 cm / 100–165 kg; women 175–210 cm / 85–145 kg. These are approved typical adult production ranges, not hard biological limits.

## 3. Male Anatomy

Heavy chest, strong shoulders, pronounced neck muscles, angular horn or crest structures and thick tail base.

## 4. Female Anatomy

Powerful but somewhat more tapered upper body, broad pelvis, distinct horn/crest variation and robust tail; do not add mammalian breasts as a default.

## 5. Shared Physical Traits

Scaled hide, clawed five-fingered hands, reptilian snout and teeth, visible tail, external ear openings rather than human ears; **no wings**; no universal breath weapon.

## 6. Coloration & Variation

Rust, ocher, red-brown, slate, green, bronze, dark umber and muted ivory scales; patterned ventral scales and horns; eye color variable.

## 7. Lifespan & Development

**Typical lifespan:** 75–110 years. Adult in late teens to early 20s; aging slower than humans but much shorter than elves.

## 8. Physical Capabilities

Strong frame and gripping capability; tail may aid balance; no automatic breath weapon, flight or magical aptitude.

## 9. Physiology

Air-breathing omnivores; terrestrial physiology; scale texture should not imply invulnerability.

## 10. Temperament & Psychology

No innate militarism, honor code or authoritarian disposition; Velmora's civic institutions are cultural.

## 11. Visual Identification

Clearly reptilian face, scales, tail and horn/ridge system; no wings or fire effects unless separately authorized.

## 12. Character Generation

Vary horn shape, scale color, facial proportions, body mass, age and markings.

## 13. Art Production Guidelines

Need explicit orthographic anatomy for feet, hands, tail and horn placement before image batch production.

# A13 — Tortle

## 1. Ancestry Overview

**Classification:** Tortle. Upright turtle-like humanoids with integral dorsal shell, broad plastron, thick limbs, beaked face and sturdy neck.

## 2. Physical Dimensions

**Typical adult dimensions:** Men 155–185 cm / 90–170 kg; women 150–180 cm / 85–160 kg. These are approved typical adult production ranges, not hard biological limits.

## 3. Male Anatomy

Heavy limbs, thick neck, wide shoulders and pronounced shell mass; beak and head shape vary.

## 4. Female Anatomy

Similarly shell-bearing and sturdy, with modest average differences in shoulder and limb mass; no mammalian breasts by default.

## 5. Shared Physical Traits

Keratinous beak, scaled or pebbled skin, thick fingers and toes with blunt claws, dorsal shell and ventral plastron; no external human ears.

## 6. Coloration & Variation

Olive, moss, umber, tan, gray and deep green skin; shells brown, ocher, olive, charcoal or mottled; varied natural scute patterns.

## 7. Lifespan & Development

**Typical lifespan:** 70–120 years (Erf canon; differs from some D&D editions). Adult in late teens to 20s; slow visible aging; Erf lifespan intentionally longer than the short tortle lifespan in some D&D material.

## 8. Physical Capabilities

Stable posture and substantial protective shell; less trunk flexibility and potentially less agility in tight spaces.

## 9. Physiology

Air-breathing terrestrial or semi-aquatic omnivores, competent swimmers who must surface for air; shell is permanent living anatomy, not removable equipment. Partial head/limb retraction is possible; complete withdrawal is not.

## 10. Temperament & Psychology

No innate patience, wisdom or slowness of thought.

## 11. Visual Identification

Shell/plastron must be anatomically integral; avoid a costume-like detachable shell or human torso with shell strapped on.

## 12. Character Generation

Vary shell shape, pattern, beak, limb mass, skin color and age; no required sailor identity.

## 13. Art Production Guidelines

Front/back/side anatomy references showing shell junctions, tail if any, and harness clearance.

## Cross-Ancestry Production Protocol

1. Select ancestry and, where applicable, adapted population before generating anatomy.
2. Generate sex, adult age, height, body proportions, skin/scales, hair, eyes, distinctive structures, and individual temperament independently.
3. Render a **neutral anatomy portrait** before applying clothing, heraldry, occupational equipment or national styling.
4. Validate hands, ears, horns, tusks, gills, shell, tail, and body proportions against the relevant entry.
5. Apply nationality, cultural presentation, profession and racing affiliation from the separate Eight-Nation Canon.
6. Maintain a stable appearance seed / character ID for repeat illustrations; never reroll foundational anatomy between images.
7. Keep biological tendencies separate from existing 0–100 gameplay attributes and AI personality traits.

## Canon Decisions Locked for v1.0

1. The v0.1 typical adult dimension, lifespan and maturity ranges are accepted as production ranges, not rigid limits.
2. **Female Hill Dwarves never grow facial hair.** **Female Mountain Dwarves never grow full beards**; exaggerated sideburns and hair along the jawline and chin are possible, but not mandatory.
3. Dragonkin have plantigrade clawed feet, five-digit clawed hands, paired cranial horns or ridges with individual variation, a long muscular tail, no wings and no universal breath weapon. Female Dragonkin do not have mammalian breasts by default.
4. Sea Elves are amphibious, breathing air, freshwater and saltwater; paired subtle neck gills, partial finger/toe webbing and good underwater vision are standard. Normal physical pressure/depth limitations apply.
5. Tortles live typically 70–120 years on Erf; permanent living shell/plastron, partial but not complete shell retraction, five functional digits, competent swimming and air breathing.
6. Forest Elf fae associations are anatomical/aesthetic, not an automatic grant of magical abilities.
7. Temperament and personality are generated independently of ancestry, without species-wide stereotypes.
8. Generated characters retain consistent anatomy across portraits and other assets; culture and clothing are applied separately.

## Production Notes Still Open

- Detailed orthographic artwork and model-sheet validation for each ancestry.
- Numeric gameplay balancing remains separate; no new ancestry-specific attribute bonuses are established.
- Rare individual traits, mutations and supernatural exceptions require separate approval.
