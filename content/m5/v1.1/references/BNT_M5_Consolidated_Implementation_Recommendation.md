### Required seams

- Persistence: new current-state validator/command wrappers, explicit checkpoint version, paged history and record-ref IDB roots; preserve sync domain contracts with awaited coordinator, not async setItem masquerade.
- Generation: reuse16 core factories/name/profile contracts, independent domain seeds/committed epochs; fixed persistent birth and scoped locals; no player adaptation.
- Competition: versioned trusted offer/definition producer; separate competition identity/opportunity/instance from mechanical context; extend exact schedule adapter for surfaces, width and presentation while keeping native setup/clearance/scoring.
- Economy: Home/Regional batch identity and native market.acquire/capacity/contract terms; narrow intermission permission only, no roster swap/treatment loophole. Preserve existing M4 effects and disclose full wage terms.
- Presentation: reuse Events tiles/Details/Registration/Current Championship/Last Event, renderer/map/radio; add region/tier/venue and persistent/local identity projection. Save/load remains existing Options workflow.

## Content production readiness

Begin supplemental authoring now:16 prestige venue concepts, regional venue/environment/naming profiles, event/schedule/tier bindings, hybrid population/local policies and presentation. Actual geometry references and source ranges are verified; no new physics required. Provisional content must not be stamped approved while the material policy fields remain unresolved. Keep candidate v1.0 unchanged; publish additions/amendments under a new version with hashes and cross-reference compiler.

Do not make full procedural circuit generation, travel economy, snow/ice mechanics, variable road width, independent rivals, player scaling, runtime LLMs, replay archives or unique art prerequisites. Proper names/shared placeholders/native courses are enough for a playable M5.

## Material owner dispositions before production

1. Generated competition wins' eligibility for named trophies/victory unlocks; explicit Completion Bonus/award context, with1-event or altered race counts requiring a real approved economic binding.
2. Persistent rival condition between championships: abstract healthy native baseline (recommended) versus retained injury/fatigue, without offscreen medical simulation or invented permanent losses.
3. Exact renewal handling after zero-player-start voluntary withdrawal: retain opportunity batch and/or Home inventory versus renewal only after terminal AI schedule. Full between-champ Home renewal and anti-reroll objectives must be reconciled explicitly.
4. Veteran Recruiters under renewable inventory: its original one extra Premium candidate in a fixed Market must not silently become an unapproved recurring grant or disappear.
5. Authorize bounded M5-A current-state/record format and compact historical fidelity/development-save transition. No unchanged-format deletion, limit increase or silent save migration.

Ordinary schema keys/module boundaries/independent RNG/caches/retry limits/asset reuse need no extra owner votes. Home nation selection is not required: use a neutral existing Home Market mix initially. If making player-selected home nation mechanically bias inventory, obtain an explicit player-facing creation/location contract; do not invent one as a dependency. Geographic national→international schedule weighting is a content proposal, not new travel costs or format unlocks.

## Acceptance stop boundary

Each delivery freezes exact tested source/artifact/evidence and awaits owner review under its future authorization. New physics/economic formulas, unsupported geometry, unsafe compaction, ambiguous once-only effects or inability to recover durable authority require stop/report. No E5/E6-style scope expansion, commit/merge/push/deploy occurs in this investigation.
