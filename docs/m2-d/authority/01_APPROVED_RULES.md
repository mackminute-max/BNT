# M2-D — Frozen Gameplay and Accounting Rules

## Championship boundary
One cycle per authoritative completed championship when returning home; no cycle for individual races or departure. State flow: active -> completed -> settlement preview -> confirmed atomic settlement -> stable preparation. Preview is pure. A terminal withdrawal/disqualification can settle only with an authoritative completion record. Until a live championship controller exists, production gameplay must not expose a fake settlement/reward trigger; fixture-driven tests may exercise the subsystem.

## Payroll and arrears
On confirmed settlement, credit only validated authoritative championship income, pay oldest outstanding claims, assess and pay current contractual wages for all active drivers/specialists (reserves included), record remaining arrears; treasury >=0. Gold is exact integer, no interest. Old claims ordered by originating cycle, personnel ID, obligation ID. Current wages ordered by personnel ID. An employee hired during stable preparation is first charged on the next championship return, not immediately. Contract with one cycle remaining receives final wage before decrement/expiry. Claims survive termination, expiry, departure and confirmed career failure as historical liabilities. Voluntary partial/full payments are separate protected actions during stable preparation.

## Debt state
Counter = consecutive confirmed settlements closing with positive arrears: 0 Current, 1 Overdue (warning), 2 Delinquent (-5%), 3 Critical (-10%), 4+ Default (-15%). Paying all arrears during preparation immediately resets status/counter and removes restrictions/penalty. Partial payment does not reset positive-debt streak. Next settlement with unpaid wages starts again at Overdue. Never increment on UI refresh, race, departure or duplicate settlement.

At Delinquent+: block recruitment, beast/chariot/equipment purchases, facility upgrades, training (including XP-funded), Gold engineering and other discretionary expansion via authoritative quotes AND reducers. Allow medical treatment, driver recovery, beast care, chariot repair/refurbishment, legal asset sales, arrears payment, and legal personnel termination, with existing costs/validation. At Critical/Default also block ordinary contract renewals. No facility upkeep in M2-D.

## Morale effect
Authorized additive, per-entrant, replay-serialized payroll-debt racing context. Snapshot the stable's status at championship entrant commitment; do not alter it mid-championship if preparation state later changes. Apply status multiplier (1.00, .95, .90, .85) to effective driver **control** after native derivation, clamped to legal effective-control range, on every derivation path; do not mutate native ratings, native condition/strain, crowd/weather effects, personality or opponents. Existing neutral/no-context behavior must be bit-identical to historical replays. Record versioned context in setup/replay; if actual engine's control definition/range differs, stop and report rather than guessing. No independent specialist morale modifier in M2-D.

## Contracts
After settlement wage assessment, decrement term once. Zero -> expired-pending-decision, not entry-eligible. During stable preparation player may explicitly select Short (3 cycles, 1.25 wage multiplier, 0 signing), Standard (8, 1.00, 50 signing), or Long (16, .85, 125 signing), using existing M2-C tier/quality wage rules and capacity checks. Renewal creates a new versioned contract identity and pays signing charge via protected transaction; do not mutate historical contract identity or forgive old debt. Player may instead release employee, preserving archived identity/claims. Never auto-renew. Critical/Default block renewal. Assigned expired personnel remain visible but are entry-ineligible; release must explicitly resolve assignment without dangling references, with clear preview/confirmation.

## Default departures
First Default status is at the fourth consecutive unpaid settlement; **first automatic departure opportunity is the fifth consecutive unpaid settlement**. At most one employee per subsequent unpaid settlement. Eligible: active contracted employee with unpaid personal wages, not an employee currently required to preserve the last entry-eligible contracted driver, and not silently removable from an existing assignment. Sort by largest outstanding personal claim, then stable ID. Assigned candidate: defer that candidate rather than silently unassign; choose next eligible candidate in deterministic order. If no candidate is safe, record a deferred departure reason; do not force it. Historical claims remain. Explicit player release/expiry is not an automatic departure and can cause roster shortage.

## Driver-only emergency contract
Explicitly requested, maximum one active emergency driver. Eligibility: no entry-eligible driver and ordinary driver recruitment unavailable due to financial restriction, insufficient Gold, exhausted eligible offers or other validated recruitment blockage; no other existing eligible driver may be fielded. Respect normal driver capacity: if at capacity, player must explicitly free capacity by legal release/other management action first; no forced dismissal or capacity bypass. Fixed profile version `m2d-emergency-driver-v1`: five native driver ratings 40, condition 100, Healthy injury, management fatigue 0, native strain 0, XP 0, six personality tendencies 50; no bonuses/equipment. One championship-cycle term, 0 signing Gold, 20 G wage assessed at next settlement; no ordinary renewal. Stable deterministic ID per authorized emergency contract event; no market RNG consumption or market refresh. If the emergency driver is the last legal driver, roster-protection rules still apply. An emergency driver does not provide beasts/chariot/staff/gear/Gold.

## Recovery
At each confirmed settlement: driver management fatigue -20; beast native fatigue -20; Major injury -> Minor; Minor -> Healthy; Critical unchanged. Clamp at legal minimum; do not change native driver strain, chariot integrity/wear, other native fields or schema-2 behavior. No passive chariot repairs. Apply only to surviving/owned applicable records; document deterministic ordering relative to contract lifecycle.

## Stable failure — OWNER APPROVED POLICY A
Operational: legal RU can be fielded. Blocked: no currently legal RU but at least one permitted, feasible recovery action sequence remains. Failure-eligible: no legal RU AND no feasible permitted recovery sequence exists, including treatment, care, repair, lawful sales/transactions, contract renewal, normal recruitment and driver-only emergency contract. Never classify as failed merely for 0 Gold, debt, injury, expiration, or unassembled RU. Do not grant emergency assets. Failure-eligible enters `failurePending` with a clear explanation and **explicit player confirmation**; it is not terminal automatically. `confirmFailure` is a separately protected, validated transaction that marks career Failed and preserves results, obligations, assets and history. Until confirmation, user can inspect history and any permitted actions; if circumstances change and viability returns, pending failure must be revalidated/cleared. Failed careers are read-only for gameplay; export/inspection allowed. Implement conservative fail-closed assessment if viability cannot be proven exhausted: remain Blocked rather than wrongly terminal. Avoid an unbounded/unsafe exhaustive search; use a sound documented reachability predicate and test fixtures. If exact proof of irrecoverability is impossible under existing mechanics, do not expose confirmFailure and report the remaining gate rather than incorrectly fail a career.
