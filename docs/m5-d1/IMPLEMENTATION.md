Retain full current assets, finance/employment, progression, active championship frozen setup/results/pending choices and recovery authority. Retire detailed official sessions only when native validator and recovery no longer require them. Preserve current/previous complete generations. Missing/corrupt evidence fails closed; explicit previous-root recovery activates a new monotonic generation with owner confirmation and scope disclosure. Never turn completed result into DNF or restart. Preserve multiple slots, global options, identity sidecars, manual save, auto recovery, complete portable export/import with staged atomic activation and unrelated-career isolation. Clearly distinguish incomplete exports or simplify to complete export. Mid-race close restarts original seed at tick0, discards in-race Signs; no per-tick writes.
