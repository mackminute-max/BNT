## Workload and controls

Before the formal study, run 15 pilot races (one per cohort/weather at r=0), excluded from formal counts. Freeze the following target unless a resource or integrity blocker requires a documented revision: **600 Combat races, 200 per cohort**, plus **120 matched Pure controls, 40 per cohort**. Forty numerical seed blocks (4100..4139), each repeated across all five approved weather presets and all three cohorts. Pure controls use r divisible by five (8 seed blocks × five weather). Weather is balanced, not sampled from an assumed real-world frequency. Healthy and mixed cohorts include all conditions to permit matching; the high-risk cohort is deliberately stressed equipment, not a claim about ordinary entrants.

Within each cohort, r parity assigns neutral vs composite degraded surfaces (grip .7, roughness .5, rolling resistance 1.1, hazard weight 1). Floor(r/2) parity assigns ordinary row separation 2 vs spread rows separated by 6 tiles; columns remain aligned, stationary, four lanes, eight entrants, default three laps/common finish line. Spread rows are a traffic proxy, not proof of continuously open track. Rotation r mod 8 rotates eight stable RU identities through all slots five times. Treatment combinations are fixed strata, not randomized independent effects. Same r/seed pairs cohorts/formats/weather; do not treat 1600 RU entries as independent casualty trials.

Healthy: default graded RU attributes 46..60, original generated personalities, 100 condition/no wear/default chariots. Mixed: equal exposure to light/heavy/protected/exposed equipment, chariot condition 100/85/70/55, wear 0/10/20/30, alternating aggressive/cautious profiles. High-risk: chariot condition 20/27/34/41, wear 60, driver condition 65, beasts 70, aggressive profiles, driving 25..60, handling 30..65, awareness 25..60, nerve 30..65, stability 20..55. These are legal initially eligible entrants, with no injected casualty or damage event. Not comparable resource budgets or isolated attribute effects: cohort differences are composite stress tests. All exact components/configs/seeds are exported. High-risk clear weather/neutral strata serve as matched reference contexts; dangerous presets and degraded surfaces supply environmental stress without modifying coefficients.

## Mechanism panels (reported separately)

AI panel: legal attack+ram, weapon only, neither, heavy/light opponent, high-A/low-O, low-A/high-O, damaged, Out of Control and valuable racing-position contexts. With default ranges, ram-only is mathematically impossible (ram legality is a subset); report that rather than changing ranges. Evaluate seeds 1..64 per context, save candidates/terms, intended/effective/physical records and 5-second follow-up consequences. Diagnostic starting speeds/close positions are not standing-start race evidence. Extra goal-persistence probes are forced-state diagnostics.

Physical panel: commanded attack/ram/no-offense matched duels; mass, closing speed, protection, stability/durability/maneuverability, driving/handling/combat/awareness/nerve, condition and contact geometry varied. Commands establish availability, not AI selection. Repeat default coefficients with legal attributes; no damage or defense tuning. Use existing resolver for every consequence. Forced-state accumulation/OOC/wreck tests are labeled, separate from full-race casualty estimates. Repeated legal attacks/ram commands respect cooldown, use reference drivers to isolate resolution. Wreck persistence and secondary-contact evidence must identify which initial state was forced.

## Measurements

Reuse the Phase 2 authoritative engine runner and reporting, adding external event readers only. Save race/entry metrics, candidate counts/scores, ram opportunities (AI decisions containing legal ram candidates, not time spent in range), intended/effective actions, physical attack/ram contests and success, reactions and defense margins, damage by cause/component, minimum observed condition, threshold crossings, control states, incidents/wreck impacts, official classification and fatigue. Existing sampled speed/blocked/overtake definitions remain as documented in Phase 2.

Serious damage is an **analysis definition**, not a game rule: any component crosses from >25 to <=25 condition during competition. Also export end-condition <=25 separately to avoid labeling initially worn entrants as newly seriously damaged. Default disablement thresholds remain 5 driver/chariot and all beasts <=10, or instability >=100. Out of Control requires instability >40. Record command diagnostics separately from emergent choices.

For every contest, subtract only the actual logged reactive bonus (10 plus 12 when defending) from logged defense to calculate an arithmetic same-state/no-reaction threshold contrast. This is not a counterfactual full race, damage saved cannot be recovered for defeated contests without altering component draws, and no fake avoided-damage number will be invented. Reaction-budget exhaustion is observed subsequent attack with reacted=false; label OOC-ineligible cases separately where observable. Immediate avoided damage has zero reliable component-level measurement; report threshold flips and the bounded nominal attack amount instead.
