Main study: **600 Combat races, 200 per cohort**, with **120 matched Pure controls**. Separate controlled-personality panel: **100 Combat races**. Diagnostics: 768 five-second AI probes, 1824 five-second commanded physical duels, 768 simultaneous two-attacker groups, 720 imposed-exposure series, and 480 two-entry moving-start complete diagnostic races (96 original +384 follow-up). Fifteen pilots are excluded from study totals. None of the forced/commanded diagnostic outcomes is pooled into natural ordinary-field casualty rates.

Healthy/mixed/high-risk Combat entries have respectively 0/5/82 damage retirements out of 1600 each. No rams are autonomously selected in the main cohorts; low-Opportunism diagnostic drivers can select them without tuning. The three clearance stalls are disclosed, reproduced and retain locked official classification. No integrity errors occurred in the cohort/personality workloads. Current mechanics demonstrate much of the condition-dependent danger target, but healthy ordinary serious risk and useful autonomous ram tactics remain unproven.

All machine-readable inputs, actual outputs, complete relevant events, replay evidence, tests, baseline and checksums are in this directory. Historical Phase 2/audit files remain unchanged. No final owner acceptance, new browser/offline verification, commit, push, publication or deployment is claimed by this investigation.
