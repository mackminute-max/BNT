## Availability and identity semantics

- Economy migration creates the same23 approved offers. Recruitment/purchase uses the existing quote, capacity, signing/wage and protected transaction rules. Sold/consumed offers are not replenished.
- Names, ancestry and biographies are presentation metadata. A real offer ID, or an owned asset's persisted economics.sourceOfferId, resolves the same profile after export/import/reload. No extra fields are injected into strict save/native schemas; raw stored names and historical receipts remain unchanged. Older builds may show generic names.
- Attributes, condition, prices, tiers and quality are generated by the existing deterministic market for the career ID. They are **not** prescribed by the biographies. Driver temperament/personality is not changed; there are no ancestry modifiers, racial bonuses or new qualifications.
- Paid owned assets remain actual native RU/training/service inputs. Browser proof purchases Cinder, reloads it and commits it into a legal ordered two-beast RU.
- No future-unlock content is fabricated. Broader targets need an approved versioned market/content policy before expansion; E5-B/E5-C remain separate.
